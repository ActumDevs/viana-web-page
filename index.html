07 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical
questions.

Ask us something else
Can I inspect the blocks or machines before buying?

Yes. We welcome visits by appointment and can arrange a live video inspection. We can also send additional photographs, measurements and condition information.

Do you arrange international delivery?

Yes. We coordinate transport worldwide — from loading at the quarry to port and final delivery — and keep you updated at every step.

Can you source a specific marble or machine?

Often, yes. Tell us the material or model you are looking for and we will check our yard and trusted partners for a match.

How do I receive a quotation?

Send us your requirements through the inquiry form or by email and you will receive a clear, itemised quotation within one working day.
